Hero memory match

A small memory game made with JavaScript. Eight cards, four characters. How few moves will you need to find every pair?

How to play

  1. Click "Start game" to start the timer.
  2. Turn over two cards at a time. Matching characters stay face up; other cards turn back over.
  3. Find all four pairs to win. Aim for fewer moves, or click "Restart" to try a new round.
